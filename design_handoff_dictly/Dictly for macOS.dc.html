macOS — 21 artboards
Menu bar app, no Dock icon, no accounts. The custom keyboard becomes a held fn key and a floating panel at the bottom of the screen. Same lime-on-black system as iOS.

2.6 Permissions — three switches, one screen
Three permissions
macOS asks once each. You'll never see this screen again.
Microphone
To hear you. Audio goes to the provider you chose, nowhere else.
Granted
Accessibility
To type the finished text where your cursor is.
Granted
Input Monitoring
To notice the fn key being held. Nothing else is read.
Open Settings
What Dictly does with this
Listens while you hold fn. Sends that audio to your provider. Types the result back.
What it never does
Log your keystrokes. Read your screen. Send anything to a Dictly server — there isn't one.
Dictly re-checks by itself. Come back to this window when the switch is on.
