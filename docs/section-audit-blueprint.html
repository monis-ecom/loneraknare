The three real gaps

Everything a section.store landing page can do that yours literally can't today.
gap 01 · list building

Email capture

No inline opt-in exists in the plugin. This is the single highest-ROI miss — an email list is the one marketing asset you own outright, and it powers every abandoned-cart and launch email later.

gap 02 · lead gen

Contact / lead form

No structured form for quotes, wholesale, consults or support. Right now those come in as scattered DMs and emails — pure context-switching tax for a solo operator.

gap 03 · social proof

Instagram / UGC feed

No customer-content wall. UGC is cheap, high-trust proof — and once wired up it refreshes itself instead of you hand-updating a testimonials block.

Why this set builds a high-converting landing page

The conversion spine you'll have

  • Hook — hero + announcement/urgency (have)
  • Show — gallery, before/after, video, hotspots (have + new gallery)
  • Prove — reviews, testimonials, UGC feed, trust badges (have + new social feed)
  • Answer — FAQ, comparison, size chart (have)
  • Offer — bundles, quantity breaks, countdown (have)
  • Capture — email + lead form (the new part)
  • Close — sticky ATC + guarantee (have)

Why it's built for solo + ADHD

  • Short critical path — only 2 must-build widgets; everything else is optional.
  • Reuse over rebuild — presets + starter pages mean you assemble, not author.
  • Fewer decisions — brand tokens set look once, globally.
  • Visible momentum — this tracker's ring rewards each finished piece.
  • Self-refreshing proof — UGC/reviews update themselves.
