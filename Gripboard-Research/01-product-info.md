## 2. How It Works

### Technical version
The Gripboard is a cutting/preparation board with **integrated food-fixation hardware**: a set of upward-pointing **stainless-steel spikes (piggar)** grip and hold food (vegetables, bread, meat) in place, **raised corner walls/edges (kanter)** brace items so they can't slide off, and **non-slip rubber feet / suction (gummifötter)** anchor the board to the counter. This lets a user peel, slice, spread butter, and cut **using only one hand** — the board performs the "second hand's" job of holding and stabilising the food.

### Simplified version (grade-6 level)
Normally you need two hands in the kitchen — one to hold the food, one to cut. If one hand doesn't work well (or at all), everything slips and slides and you can't do it. The Gripboard has little metal spikes that hold the food still for you and grippy feet so the board stays put. So you can chop, peel and butter bread with just one hand — on your own, without asking for help.
