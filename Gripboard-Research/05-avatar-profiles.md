## MAIN AVATAR DESIRE (the one driver above all)

> **"Jag vill kunna klara mig själv i köket — laga min egen mat, med den hand jag har — utan att be om hjälp och utan att känna mig som en börda."**
>
> **To keep my independence and dignity in the most everyday act — feeding myself — without having to ask for help.**

Everything else (safety, gift-giving, re-enablement) serves this. Backed by real signal: *"måltiden [blev] en symbol för självständighet, att klara sig själv"* and *"oro för att bli en börda"* [SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Äldre i centrum / Kunskapsguiden].

**Note on the buyer vs. user split:** the *user's* desire is independence; the *gift-buyer's* desire is to grant that independence (and worry less). Copy must often speak to both at once.

## SUB-AVATAR 1 — "Den självständiga 74-åringen" (Elderly self-buyer, aching hands)

- **Who:** 70–82, lives alone or with an equally-aged partner, still in their own home, proud, private.
- **Trigger:** hands have slowly gotten weaker/achier (artros); the carrot rolls, the bread slides, cutting has become a daily small humiliation.
- **Emotions:** frustration, quiet grief at losing a skill, refusal to "make a fuss", fear of the day they can't stay home.
- **Behaviour:** researches carefully, price-sensitive but will pay for something that *works* and lasts; distrusts hype; may buy from a specialist (Seniorlife/Varsam) or be shown it by family.
- **Real language:** *"skala och skära"*, *"klara mig själv"*, *"glider iväg"*.
- **What wins them:** understated proof, stability/safety, "still a normal kitchen tool." **Avoid** anything that screams "old" or "disabled."

## SUB-AVATAR 2 — "Den oroliga dottern/sonen" (Adult-child gift-buyer) — HIGHEST COMMERCIAL VALUE

- **Who:** 40–58, lives in another town from an ageing parent; time-poor, guilt-carrying, has money.
- **Trigger:** noticed at a visit that Mum/Dad struggles to cut food, or a parent's stroke/diagnosis; wants to *do something* concrete.
- **Emotions:** love + guilt + helplessness at distance; wants to help without wounding the parent's pride.
- **Behaviour:** shops online, buys as a **gift** (birthday/Christmas), converts fast if the story is clear and it looks caring-not-clinical, responds to Meta/Instagram ads.
- **What wins them:** the gift narrative (*"ge tillbaka självständigheten"*), safety reassurance, easy ordering + delivery, a product that won't insult the recipient.
- **Why prioritise:** highest willingness-to-pay, easiest to reach on paid social, buys for others (repeat/seasonal). **This is the primary ad avatar.**
- **Quote:** `[behöver verifieras]`

## SUB-AVATAR 3 — "Strokeöverlevaren" (Stroke survivor, one working arm)

- **Who:** 60–80 (skews 70+ per Socialstyrelsen), months–years post-stroke, one arm weak/paralysed, in or past rehab.
- **Trigger:** discharged home; relearning daily tasks; an OT may have shown them a "fixplatta"/pin-board.
- **Emotions:** determination + fatigue; every regained task is a victory against the stroke; hates dependence.
- **Behaviour:** guided by arbetsterapeut and family; the aid may be prescribed or family-bought; strongly motivated once shown it works one-handed.
- **Real signal [SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Strokeförbundet]:** for a *"förlamad arm"*, *"stålpiggarna kan hålla fast tex grönsaker som du vill skära."*
- **What wins them:** re-enablement story (03 #5), demonstrable one-handed use, credibility/OT alignment.
- **Quote:** `[behöver verifieras]`

## SUB-AVATAR 4 — "Reumatikern i arbetsför ålder" (Younger arthritis/RA user) — MARKET EXPANDER

- **Who:** 45–65, working or active, rheumatoid arthritis or hand osteoarthritis; **does not identify as elderly or disabled.**
- **Trigger:** flare-ups make gripping and cutting painful on bad days; wants to keep cooking normally without pain.
- **Emotions:** doesn't want to feel "old before their time"; resents needing an "aid"; pragmatic about pain relief.
- **Behaviour:** active in communities (e.g. Facebook *"Vi som lever med reumatism"*), researches, shares recommendations, buys online for themselves.
- **Why they matter:** largest, least-stigmatised, younger-skewing segment → lets the brand escape the "senior hjälpmedel" ghetto (03 #2). Best served by the **"verktyg, inte hjälpmedel"** identity.
- **What wins them:** dignity/normal-tool framing, "for good days and bad days" (dual-disc), peer/community proof.
- **Quote:** `[behöver verifieras]`

## (Secondary) SUB-AVATAR 5 — "Arbetsterapeuten" (Clinical recommender)

Not a volume buyer but a **trust multiplier**. Recommends aids to enable kvarboende. Wins on safety, simplicity, evidence-aligned language. Worth a dedicated trust asset (spec + safety), not ad spend. Flagged for completeness.

## NEW IDENTITY signals (for Step 2 Brand Naming & Step 5 Ads)

- The winning identity is **capable, independent, dignified — NOT patient/senior/disabled.** The brand should feel like a **smart everyday kitchen tool** with warmth, not a medical device. (Directly answers *"kan väcka skam"*.)
- Tribal belonging cue: *"de som fortfarande gör det själva"* — people who keep doing things themselves.
- Tone: Swedish understatement, warm, matter-of-fact, proof-led. No pity, no hype.
- Naming direction (for Step 2): original English brand name + Swedish product title (e.g. category words *skärbräda / enhandsbräda / köksbräda*), avoiding the clinical *"hjälpmedel"* register and **not** copying any competitor name (Fixbrädan, etc.).
