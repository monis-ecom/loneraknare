# 04 – New Information Research: Gripboard

> Goal: authority-building facts, recent Scandinavian data, and trends competitors in the adaptive-kitchen space aren't using. Used at Stage 3–5 sophistication to earn belief through **education**, not louder claims. Source-labelling per `01-product-info.md`.

---

## A. The demographic wave (the "why now" macro fact)

- **Ageing Sweden:** the population is ageing and policy strongly favours **kvarboende** (ageing in place) over institutional care. Arbetsterapi research this session [SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — DiVA/Umeå/Lund] states aids *"möjliggör kvarboende och en självständig vardag"* and that OT interventions are *"nödvändiga och av stor vikt för att äldre personer ska kunna bo kvar hemma."*
- **Marketing use:** frame the Gripboard inside the national story — more people living at home longer means more people needing to do everyday tasks solo. This is a tailwind competitors don't reference.

---

## B. Stroke data (precise, credible, Socialstyrelsen)

- **[VERIFIED — Socialstyrelsen]** ~3 of 4 stroke cases occur in people **over 70**; only ~4% (**≈1,000 people/year**) are under 50.
- **[SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Socialstyrelsen]** stroke incidence and mortality have **roughly halved since the early 2000s** — meaning **more survivors living with after-effects** (like a weak/paralysed arm) for longer. *More survival = more people needing one-handed solutions.*
- **Marketing use:** a specific, sober statistic ("3 av 4 som drabbas är över 70") lends authority without hype — ideal for the Swedish market. Ties the product to a real, growing population.

---

## C. Hand osteoarthritis (artros) — the largest silent segment

- **[SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Reumatikerförbundet / Artrosportalen (Lund)]** artros is extremely common with age, and *"aktiviteter som belastar handled och fingrar över tid, som att skala och skära"* are precisely the tasks it makes painful — i.e. the product's exact use-cases are the clinically-named pain points.
- **[SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Joint Academy / Reumatikerlinjen]** recommended management includes **hjälpmedel** (thick-handled utensils, non-slip supports, grip aids) to *"minska belastningen på leden och minska ledsmärtan."*
- **Marketing use:** artros is a **much larger, younger-skewing, less-stigmatised** segment than "disability." Framing around *"ömma händer / artros"* widens the market beyond stroke and old age — and it's medically endorsed to use aids, which pre-empts skepticism.

## D. The professional-endorsement lever (arbetsterapeut)

- **[SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — arbetsterapi research]** the OT's role is literally to *"välja ut hjälpmedel som ska kompensera för personens avsaknad av förmåga/färdigheter."* Aids like this sit inside an established clinical prescribing pathway.
- **Marketing use:** "rekommenderas av arbetsterapeuter" style credibility (only if genuinely obtainable) is gold on a proof-first market. Even without a formal endorsement, aligning the language with OT reasoning (enable, compensate, kvarboende) borrows authority.
- **Caveat:** do **not** claim a specific endorsement, certification or medical outcome you can't document — that breaks both the law and trust. `[behöver verifieras]` for any named endorsement.

## E. Fall / injury prevention (adjacent authority)

- **[SEARCH-PARAPHRASE — Umeå kommun fall-prevention]** *"fallskador kan minskas med upp till 30 procent"*, and a fall *"är ofta grundorsak till behov av stöd och ökat beroende av andra."*
- **Marketing use (indirect):** a stable, non-sliding board reduces the small kitchen accidents and awkward reaches that precede bigger incidents. Reinforces the safety mechanism (03 #4) with a credible adjacent statistic — useful for the gift-buyer and OT audiences. Keep the claim modest (safer handling), don't overstate it as "prevents falls."

## F. Trend / "new information" angles competitors ignore

1. **"Kvarboende-rörelsen"** — the national shift to living at home longer as the product's *reason to exist now*. Nobody in the category tells this story.
2. **Survivorship paradox** — stroke deaths down → more survivors needing one-handed tools. A counter-intuitive, credible hook.
3. **Artros as the mass-market entry** — reframing the product away from "disability" toward the enormous "sore-hands with age" population.
4. **Independence as measurable wellbeing** — research frames self-managed meals as a *symbol of independence* and a driver of wellbeing; the product delivers a concrete piece of that.

## How to deploy new information (summary)

- Lead education with **one precise stat** at a time (Jantelagen: one credible number > five big claims).
- Best single fact for authority: **"3 av 4 som får stroke är över 70 — och allt fler överlever och lever vidare med en svagare arm."**
- Best market-expanding fact: **artros makes "skala och skära" the exact painful tasks** — so the product isn't just for stroke/disability, it's for anyone with ageing, aching hands.
