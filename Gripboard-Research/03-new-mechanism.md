# 03 – New Mechanism Identification: Gripboard

> Goal: 3–5 genuinely fresh **angles/mechanisms** ("why THIS works when nothing else did") that aren't tired in the Swedish adaptive-kitchen market. Each is scored and given a "why it's new" note. The underlying hardware (spikes + non-slip) is not new — the **framing, combination, and story** are where novelty is available at Stage 3–4 sophistication.

Scoring: **Novelty** (how unused in this market), **Believability** (Jantelagen-proof), **Desire-fit** (pull toward the core independence desire). Each /5.

---

## Mechanism 1 — "Den andra handen" (The Second Hand)

**The angle:** Don't sell "a board with spikes." Sell a **replacement second hand**. The whole problem of one-handed cooking is that every task assumes two hands — one to hold, one to work. The Gripboard *is* the holding hand: spikes + retaining edges + non-slip base together do exactly what a helper's hand would do.

- **Why it's new:** competitors list "piggar, halkfri, gummifötter" as separate specs. Reframing all of them as **one function — a second hand — is a mechanism story nobody in the Swedish hjälpmedel space is telling.** It converts a feature list into a single, emotionally legible promise.
- **Novelty 4/5 · Believability 5/5 · Desire-fit 5/5** → **Top pick.**
- Copy seed: *"Den håller maten så att du kan använda handen du har."*

---

## Mechanism 2 — "Ett verktyg, inte ett hjälpmedel" (A tool, not a disability aid)

**The angle:** Position by **category-shift** — this is a *smart kitchen tool* that happens to help, not a *medical hjälpmedel* that reminds you you're declining. The removable smooth disc makes it a genuinely normal cutting board when you don't need the spikes.

- **Why it's new / why it works:** the entire competitor set lives in the clinical "hjälpmedel/senior/reuma" aisle, which triggers the exact shame signal the research surfaced (*"kan väcka skam"*). Selling it as an everyday tool **removes the psychological cost of buying**. This is a *New Identity* lever (see Phase E), not just a mechanism.
- **Novelty 4/5 · Believability 4/5 · Desire-fit 5/5**
- Copy seed: *"Ser ut som vilken skärbräda som helst — gör bara mer."*

---

## Mechanism 3 — "2-i-1: piggsida + slät sida" (Swap-disc dual mode)

**The angle:** The **removable/replaceable disc** (spiked ↔ smooth) is a concrete, demonstrable mechanism: one board adapts to the task and to good days vs bad days (arthritis fluctuates). Also solves the hygiene objection (spikes are fiddly to clean → lift the disc out).

- **Why it's new:** most basic pin-boards are fixed-spike. The **modularity** is a real, provable product advantage that photographs/demos well — strong for Swedish "proof over promises."
- **Novelty 3/5 · Believability 5/5 · Desire-fit 4/5**
- Copy seed: *"Piggar när du behöver hålla fast — slät yta när du bara vill skära."*

---

## Mechanism 4 — "Stabil bas = trygg hand" (Anchored base → safe knife)

**The angle:** Lead with the **non-slip/anchoring base** as a *safety* mechanism, not a convenience. A board that *"inte glider iväg"* means the knife hand never has to fight the board — fewer slips, fewer cuts, less white-knuckle effort. Ties directly to fall/injury-prevention.

- **Why it's new in framing:** competitors mention rubber feet as a footnote; **making stability the hero** speaks to the gift-buyer and the arbetsterapeut (Groups B & C), who buy on safety. Backed by real fall-prevention research signal (injuries *"kan minskas med upp till 30 procent"*).
- **Novelty 3/5 · Believability 5/5 · Desire-fit 4/5**
- Copy seed: *"Brädan sitter still — så att du kan slappna av i handen du skär med."*

---

## Mechanism 5 — "Från att bli matad till att laga själv" (Re-enablement arc)

**The angle:** A **transformation mechanism** framed as a before/after journey: post-stroke or post-diagnosis, meals become something *done to you* (helped, waited for). The Gripboard flips one concrete task back to *done by you*. It's the smallest possible tool with an outsized dignity payoff.

- **Why it's new:** nobody sells the *emotional mechanism* — the shift in who is in control of the meal. Strong for the re-enablement sub-avatar and the gift narrative.
- **Novelty 4/5 · Believability 4/5 · Desire-fit 5/5**
- Copy seed: *"Först höll någon annan i kniven. Nu gör du det själv."*

---

## Recommendation for downstream steps

- **Primary mechanism:** #1 **"Den andra handen"** — clearest, most believable, highest desire-fit; it unifies the spec sheet into one promise.
- **Identity lever:** #2 **"Ett verktyg, inte ett hjälpmedel"** — carry into Brand Naming (Step 2) and ad angles (Step 5) to escape the shame-laden hjälpmedel framing.
- **Proof mechanic:** #3 (dual-disc) and #4 (anchored base) are the **demonstrable** claims for video/UGC — show, don't tell (Swedish "proof over promises").
- **Emotional mechanic:** #5 for the hero video / gift creative.

**Caveat:** all mechanisms assume the one-handed-cutting-board product identity and the standard feature set (spikes, removable disc, edges, non-slip). Confirm the actual Gripboard has the removable-disc feature before leaning on Mechanism #3.
