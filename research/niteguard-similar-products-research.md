## 2. What the winning ads prove about this audience

The highest-spending Swedish senior-targeted ads all follow the NiteGuard formula:

1. **Statistic or fear hook** ("7 av 10 seniorer kämpar med smärta & stelhet dagligen", "1 av 4 svenskar har artros")
2. **Problem agitation** (pain → worse tomorrow, fall risk, lost independence)
3. **Mechanism-based solution** (gel pressure distribution, compression, TENS, heat)
4. **Dignity framing** ("Det här är inte en sjukhusprodukt")
5. **Swedish trust signals** (fri frakt, Klarna/Swish, öppet köp, "1 500+ svenskar")

Every product below can be sold with this exact playbook on your existing pixel.

## 3. Product opportunities — ranked

### TIER 1 — Direct pixel match: night safety, bedroom, dignity (test first)

#### 1. Sängstödshandtag / uppresningsstöd (bedside support handle)
- **Proof:** *Handly* (handly.se) sells exactly this at **299 kr** and is one of the biggest Swedish senior-ad spenders found (~14 500+ EU spend across two top creatives). Copy: "Äldre förtjänar trygghet och komfort i varje steg."
- **Why it fits:** Identical audience and identical moment — getting out of bed at night. NiteGuard removes the toilet trip; the handle secures the trips that still happen. Perfect cross-sell/upsell AND standalone product.
- **Price:** sell 399–499 kr (undercut nothing, out-position Handly on bundle). COGS ~70–120 kr (AliExpress/CJ "bed rail assist handle").
- **Angle:** "28 % högre fallrisk vid nattliga uppstigningar — de flesta fall sker vid sängkanten."

#### 2. Rörelseaktiverad nattbelysning (motion-sensor night light kit, 3-pack)
- **Proof:** Appears in every 2026 elderly-aids bestseller list (fall prevention at night); huge US/global category; low Swedish ad saturation found = open lane.
- **Why it fits:** The literal continuation of your winning story: darkness + bad balance + half-asleep = falls. Your existing e-book "Fallskydd i Hemmet" markets it for free.
- **Price:** 299–399 kr for 3-pack (COGS 40–80 kr) → excellent margin, light shipping.
- **Angle:** "Mörkret är den verkliga fallrisken. Ljuset tänds innan foten når golvet."

#### 3. Vattentätt madrasskydd premium-set
- **Proof:** *Hetetop* running Swedish ads at **479 kr** (quilted waterproof mattress protector + pillowcase set, ~1 400 spend and active).
- **Why it fits:** Nocturia/incontinence-adjacent, dignity + hygiene framing ("Rent & Fräscht" — you already sell the hygiene guide). Zero-embarrassment marketing possible ("skyddar mot spill, svett och olyckor").
- **Price:** 399–549 kr. COGS 90–150 kr.

#### 4. Dörr-/sänglarm eller trygghetsalarm för anhöriga (caregiver motion/bed alarm)
- **Proof:** Global category winner (fall prevention, dementia care); adult-children buyers convert strongly ("gifts for aging parent" behavior documented in dropship data).
- **Price:** 499–799 kr. Sell to the *anhörig* side of your pixel (your "Trygg Natt — för anhöriga" guide proves you already reach them).

## 4. Watch-outs

- **Hearing amplifiers** (Nartulas "osynlig hörapparat" 997–1 089 kr, heavy spend; Helios Hear advertorial vs "Audika 22 000 kr") — proven demand but **regulatory/claims risk** in Sweden (hörapparat = medical device). Only enter with "hörselförstärkare" wording and legal review.
- **Creams/supplements with medical claims** (e.g. "Svenska Läkaresällskapet"-branded bone cream ads are fake-endorsement scams) — do not imitate; Meta bans follow.
- **Compression socks** — profitable but the most crowded lane found; enter only with the sock-aid bundle differentiator.
